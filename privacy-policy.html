Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This app ("Camila Pipeline") is a personal content-automation tool operated by a single developer. It connects, via the official Meta/Instagram Graph API, to one Instagram Business account that the developer owns and controls, in order to publish content and manage comments on that account.

What data this app accesses

The app accesses only data belonging to the connected Instagram Business account: its own media (photos, videos, captions) and the public comments left on that account's own posts. It does not access data belonging to any other Instagram account or user profile.

What the app does with this data

  • Publishes photo, carousel, and video content to the connected account.
  • Reads public comments on the connected account's own posts and, where appropriate, posts a reply.
  • Does not sell, share, or transfer any of this data to third parties.
  • Does not use this data for advertising or profiling purposes.

Data storage

Access tokens and API credentials are stored privately by the developer (locally and in a private GitHub repository's encrypted secrets store) and are not shared with any third party. Comment text processed for generating replies is sent to Google's Gemini API solely to draft a reply and is not stored by that service beyond normal API processing.

Data deletion

Since this app only operates on the developer's own account and does not collect data from other users, there is no separate end-user data to delete. To request removal of any data related to this integration, contact the address below.
